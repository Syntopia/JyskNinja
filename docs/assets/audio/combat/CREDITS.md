# Recorded combat foley

These 28 edited sound files are sourced from works published under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No purchase or subscription is required. Source licences checked September 13, 2026.

- **slash-*.wav, heavy-*.wav**: [Swish: Bamboo stick weapon swishes](https://opengameart.org/content/swish-bamboo-stick-weapon-swhoshes), Iwan “qubodup” Gabovitch. Recordings of a bamboo stick moving through air.
- **hit-*.wav**: [Punch](https://opengameart.org/content/punch), Iwan “qubodup” Gabovitch. Based on the creator’s punching-bag recording.
- **clash-*.wav**: [20 Sword Sound Effects](https://opengameart.org/content/20-sword-sound-effects-attacks-and-clashes), StarNinjas. Creator describes recording two knives against each other.
- **body-*.wav**: [37 hits/punches](https://opengameart.org/content/37-hitspunches), Independent.nu, submitted by qubodup.

Edits: mono fold-down, modest bandpass filtering, silence trimming, gain balancing, click-prevention fades, and conversion to 44.1 kHz PCM16 WAV. Playback applies subtle pitch variation; heavier swings and armour/body impacts use lower playback rates. No synthesized layer is added to these recordings.

`manifest.json` maps every output to its original source filename and SHA256 hash, with processing settings and measured levels. The separate procedural movement, death, ambience and music systems retain their existing sources and implementation.
